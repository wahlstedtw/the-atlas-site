Common Questions

Where are my maps saved?

On your device. If you are signed in to iCloud, your maps are also copied to your own iCloud Drive so they follow you to your other iPhones and iPads.

How do I move my maps to a new device?

Sign in to the same Apple Account with iCloud Drive turned on, install the game, and open it. Your maps sync in on their own.

Can I delete a map?

Yes. From the main menu, choose the map and tap Delete.

Does it need an internet connection?

No. The game plays fully offline. iCloud sync catches up the next time you are online.

Which devices are supported?

iPhone and iPad running iOS 15 or later.
